Prompts rot when they live in chat history. This idea is a library: each prompt has a job, a version, and a short note about when it fails.

The files here are starters. Replace them with the prompts you actually reuse.

A good library entry is shorter than a manifesto. It says what the model should do, what it must not invent, and what a finished answer looks like.
